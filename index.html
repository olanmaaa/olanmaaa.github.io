Background

About me

I hold a First Class B.Sc. in Microbiology from Nile University of Nigeria, where I graduated as the best student in my department. My research interests sit at the intersection of microbiology, computational biology, and public health — with a long-term goal of building a West African genomics and microbiome research institute.

I recently completed a research fellowship at Sydani Institute for Research and Innovation, where I led scoping reviews, contributed to GiveWell grant submissions, and conducted statistical reanalyses in R. I also volunteer as a biocurator for BugSigDB within the Bioconductor community.

Nigeria Immunisation Coverage Analysis

A statistical analysis of childhood immunisation coverage across Kano, Lagos, and Anambra states in Nigeria, using R to produce seven data visualisations and run Welch's ANOVA with Games-Howell post-hoc testing to identify coverage disparities across states and time.

An exploratory analysis of global malaria detection data from The Lancet, comparing PCR and microscopy diagnostic accuracy across regions including West Africa, East Africa, Asia, and South America. Built in R with regional scatter plots and a prevalence ratio boxplot.
